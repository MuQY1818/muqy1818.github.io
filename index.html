I am an incoming graduate student at Fudan TEAI and a computer science undergraduate at China University of Mining and Technology. I am currently an Embodied AI Algorithm Intern at Noematrix in Shanghai, working on embodied AI post-training.

My research explores how vision-language models reason about the physical world and guide robots through long-horizon manipulation, with an interest in spatial intelligence. Longer term, I want to work on RSI (recursive self-improvement) in Embodied AI.

Reflections

Three years ago, efficiency was the lens through which I often viewed computer science. Today, I am increasingly interested in how systems learn, reason, and act across unfamiliar situations. Looking ahead, I wonder how experience can become a lasting source of capability—how a system can learn something new without losing what it already knows.

Embodied AI gives this question a physical form. An action changes the world, and the world returns evidence that can refine the next decision. I hope to build systems that can learn from this exchange, allowing experience to shape both what they know and what they do.
