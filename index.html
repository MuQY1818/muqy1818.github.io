Hi! I am Weijue Bu, an incoming graduate student at the Institute of Trustworthy Embodied Artificial Intelligence (TEAI), Fudan University. I am currently a computer science undergraduate at China University of Mining and Technology (2023 – present), and a research intern at Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School in Prof. Zhi Wang's group, working on robotic manipulation and vision-language-model-based planning.

My research interests include embodied foundation models, embodied agents, spatial intelligence, and long-horizon robotic manipulation.

My current focus is RSI (recursive self-improvement) in Embodied AI and Agentic Embodied AI — building embodied foundation models and embodied agents that use vision-language models to drive robots through manipulation tasks.
